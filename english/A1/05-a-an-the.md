# Articles (a, an, the, zero article)

> Articles show if a noun is specific (`the`) or general/new (`a`, `an`, or no article).

## Main Rules

| Article | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `a` / `an` | singular countable, first mention, new info | `I bought a laptop.` |
| `the` | specific, both people know which one | `The laptop is fast.` |
| ∅ (zero) | general ideas, plurals, uncountables | `Dogs are loyal.` |

## a or an? (sound, not spelling)

- Consonant sound → `a`: `a book`, `a university` (/yu/), `a European`
- Vowel sound → `an`: `an apple`, `an hour` (silent h), `an MBA` (/em/)

## When to use `the`

- Second mention: `I saw a bird. The bird was yellow.`
- Unique things: `the sun`, `the moon`, `the internet`
- Superlatives: `the best`, `the biggest`
- Instruments: `play the guitar`
- Families: `the Smiths`

## When to use zero article (∅)

- General plurals: `Dogs are loyal.`
- Uncountables: `Water is important.`
- Abstract nouns: `Life is beautiful.`, `Love is more important than money.`
- Meals: `have breakfast / lunch / dinner`
- Languages: `I speak English.`
- Sports: `play soccer` (contrast: `play the guitar`)
- Most countries and cities: `Colombia`, `Bogotá` — but `the USA`

## Common Mistakes (Spanish traps)

| ❌ | ✅ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `I am doctor.` | `I am a doctor.` | professions need `a/an` |
| `The life is beautiful.` | `Life is beautiful.` | general → zero article |
| `I speak the English.` | `I speak English.` | languages → zero |
| `I play piano.` | `I play the piano.` | instruments need `the` |
| `I don't have job.` | `I don't have a job.` | countable singular needs `a` |
| `a water` | `water` / `a glass of water` | uncountable → no `a/an` |
