# Present Simple

> The present simple describes habits, routines, facts and general truths.

## Form

| Subject | Verb |
|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | base form: `work`, `study`, `go` |
| he / she / it | **+s**: `works`, `studies`, `goes` |

## Third-person spelling (`-s`)

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| most verbs: `+s` | `work → works` |
| ends in `s, sh, ch, x, o`: `+es` | `watch → watches`, `go → goes` |
| consonant + `y`: `y → ies` | `study → studies` |
| vowel + `y`: `+s` | `play → plays` |
| irregular | `have → has` |

## Uses

1. Habits / routines: `I drink coffee every morning.`
2. Facts / truths: `Water boils at 100°C.`
3. Likes / dislikes: `I like coffee.`
4. Timetables: `The train leaves at 8.`

## Negative

| Subject | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I / you / we / they | `don't` + base | `They don't work.` |
| he / she / it | `doesn't` + base | `She doesn't work.` |

## Yes/No questions

`Do` / `Does` + subject + base verb + `?`

- `Do you work here?`
- `Does she work here?`

## Short answers

| Question | Affirmative | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| `Do you work?` | `Yes, I do.` | `No, I don't.` |
| `Does she work?` | `Yes, she does.` | `No, she doesn't.` |

> Never repeat the main verb: `Yes, I do.` NOT `Yes, I work.`

## Wh- questions

`Wh-` + `do`/`does` + subject + base verb

- `Where do you live?`
- `What does she do?`

## Subject vs Object questions

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject question (wh- does the action) | `Wh-` + verb (**+s**) | `Who works here?` |
| Object question (wh- receives the action) | `Wh-` + `do/does` + subject + verb | `Who do you like?` |

## Common Mistakes (from our lessons)

| ❌ | ✅ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `He brushes his teeth and take a shower.` | `...and **takes** a shower.` | **every** verb gets its own `-s` |
| `She doesn't works.` | `She doesn't work.` | `does` already has the `-s` |
| `Does she works here?` | `Does she work here?` | same: no double `-s` |
| `What time the manager arrive?` | `What time **does** the manager arrive?` | wh- questions need `do/does` |
| `Who prepare the orders?` | `Who **prepares** the orders?` | subject question keeps the `-s` |

## Tips

1. **The `-s` lives in ONE place**: either on the verb (`works`) or on `does` (`doesn't work`). Never both.
2. **Subject question** = no `do/does`, and the verb keeps the `-s` (`Who works...?`).
3. **wh- questions replace the information** — don't repeat it in the same question.
