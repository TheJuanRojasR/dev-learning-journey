# Adverbs of Frequency

> Frequency adverbs say how often something happens. Their position depends on the verb.

## The Scale

| Adverb | Frequency |
|---|---|
| `always` | 100% |
| `usually` | 90% |
| `often` | 70% |
| `sometimes` | 50% |
| `occasionally` | 30% |
| `rarely` / `seldom` | 10% |
| `never` | 0% |

## Position — The Key Rule

| Case | Position | Example |
|---|---|---|
| normal verb | **before** the verb | `I **always** drink coffee.` |
| verb `to be` | **after** `be` | `He **is always** late.` |
| auxiliary (`don't/doesn't/can`) | **after** the auxiliary | `I **don't usually** work on Sundays.` |

## Frequency Phrases (go at the END)

`every day`, `once a week`, `twice a month`, `three times a year`

- `I go to the gym **twice a week**.`
- `She studies English **every day**.`

## Adverb vs Phrase

| Adverb (one word) | Phrase (several words) |
|---|---|
| `I **always** go to the gym.` (before the verb) | `I go to the gym **every day**.` (at the end) |

## How often...?

- `**How often** do you exercise?` → `Twice a week.`
- `**How often** does she travel?` → `Once a month.`

## Common Mistakes

| ❌ | ✅ | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `I drink always coffee.` | `I **always** drink coffee.` | before the main verb |
| `He always is late.` | `He **is always** late.` | after `be` |
| `I go every day to the gym.` | `I go to the gym **every day**.` | phrase at the end |
| `I don't never smoke.` | `I **never** smoke.` | no double negative |

## Tips

1. Two positions only: **before the main verb** or **after `be`**.
2. Phrases (`every day`, `twice a week`) go at the **end**.
3. `never` is already negative — don't add another negative.
4. Question form: `How often` + `do/does` + subject + base verb.
